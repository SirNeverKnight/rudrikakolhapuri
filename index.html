2. Genuine Leather Break-in & Oiling Tips

  • Castor/Sesame Oil Application: Apply 2-3 drops of castor or mustard oil on the underside of straps before first use to make leather soft.
  • Avoid Water Contact: Pure vegetable-tanned leather should be kept dry. If exposed to moisture, allow to air-dry in shade.
  • Natural Softening: Within 2 days of wearing, the leather sole absorbs your body heat and molds naturally to your foot shape.
